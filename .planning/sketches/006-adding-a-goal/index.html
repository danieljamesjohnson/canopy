The constraint behind the question. On 2026-09-08 the unguided add-goal field was deleted because it created goals with a 3.0 hrs/week budget nobody chose — that is how a goal called “help” became a three-hour weekly commitment. A is that same mechanism with a nicer front end. B and C both make you say the number. The trade is purely taps: B is 2 taps per goal, C is 1 tap per goal plus one shared form pass.
