## Tech Debt (non-blocking)

1. **Outstanding human UAT (cross-cutting).** 4 of 5 phases carry `human_needed` items — all on-device iOS/visual behaviors not exercisable on Linux: notification firing & tap-nav, day-boundary rollover on resume, deferred carry-in across a real day boundary, goal-form cursor feel, priority control render, donut visual correctness.
