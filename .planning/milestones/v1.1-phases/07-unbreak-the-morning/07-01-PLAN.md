<objective>
Fix the four loop-plumbing defects that let the morning loop dead-end on cold launch or resume: B1 (generation reads empty in-memory goal/commitment lists — LOOP-01), B2 (no day rollover — LOOP-02), B4 (notification never auto-schedules and its tap handler crashes under go_router — LOOP-04), and the goal-form cursor-jump bug (LOOP-05).

Purpose: The scheduling engine must always receive the user's real saved data, the schedule must roll over at the day boundary, and the daily notification must fire and navigate without crashing. These are wiring fixes that mirror patterns already proven elsewhere in the codebase (ThemeNotifier rollover, ScheduleNotifier startup init).

Output: Goals/Commitments notifiers loaded at startup (with an additive injectable-repository seam for testability), a date-aware ScheduleNotifier with a resume rollover hook, go_router-correct + auto-scheduled morning notifications, and a goal form whose TextEditingControllers live in State.
</objective>
