<objective>
Close the three Today-screen presentation gaps from the Phase 23 UAT.

**G-04 (bug).** Time-gutter labels clip at the edge. Root cause (`23-GAP-ANALYSIS.md`):
`TimelineRowTile` puts the label in a `SizedBox(width: kGutterWidth)` as the first child of a `Row`
with **zero** leading inset, and the scroll region has no horizontal padding of its own — while the
header, the edge-state line and `FreeTimeRow` all inset by 16. The gutter is the one thing on the
screen sitting at x=0.

**G-02 (design).** A 25-minute long break renders at the same visual weight as a 5-minute short
break. Phase 22-02 deliberately collapsed both into one `_buildBreak`; today the *only* differences
are the title string and the numeral. Dan wants a long break to look big. The dashed-outline
vocabulary stays, and the standing prohibition on any collapse/accordion affordance stays — size and
prominence are fine, a NEW INTERACTION is not.

**G-06 (polish).** The chunk count renders twice: "0 of 9 Chunks" in the progress row and
"Sunny day · 9 chunks" in the mood chip. Keep the progress row (it is strictly richer — completed,
total, and a bar) and drop the suffix from the chip. Per the gap analysis this diverges from the
example string locked in `22-UI-SPEC.md`, so amend that line rather than leave the spec describing a
chip that no longer exists.

Purpose: three small honesty-of-presentation defects on the screen this milestone exists to build.
Output: aligned gutter, a long break that reads long, and one count in one place.
</objective>
