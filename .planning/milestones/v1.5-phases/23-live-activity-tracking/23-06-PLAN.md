<objective>
Close **G-05**. Dan: *"Once I've completed a task I should be in 'break'."* His decision at the
sign-off gate, recorded verbatim in `23-UAT.md`, is **extend the break to fill**:

> a work chunk scheduled 10:00–10:25 with a 5-min break 10:25–10:30. Complete it at 10:10 and the
> following break absorbs the reclaimed time — it starts now (10:10) and still ends at its original
> end (10:30), becoming a 20-minute break. **Nothing downstream shifts.**

This dissolves the conflict `23-GAP-ANALYSIS.md` flagged. The analysis worried that showing a break
as "now" before its window opened would reopen the Phase 17 invariant
(`test/screens/today_screen_now_state_test.dart:470-487` — *"KEY INVARIANT: an unopened break window
is never promoted to Active"*). Under Dan's model the break's window **genuinely does** open at
10:10, because we move it. `resolveNowState` then returns `Active(break)` through the existing,
unmodified path, and `_liveSecondsRemaining`'s "the window is already open" assumption stays true.

**The invariant and its test are not to be weakened, reworded, or deleted.** This plan changes the
write side (`ScheduleNotifier.markComplete`) only. `lib/screens/today/now_state.dart` and
`lib/screens/today/today_screen.dart` receive **zero** changes.

Purpose: finishing early should hand the user their rest, not drop them into a neutral gap banner.
Output: a bounded, guarded, reversible mutation on completion, pinned by unit tests that assert both
the new behaviour and every case where it must do nothing.
</objective>
