# Plan 26-07 — Confine the now chip to the time gutter (G-01)

## Objective

Close `26-UAT.md` G-01: the now-line's `Now · <time>` chip paints over the chunk card beneath it,
occluding the title and the rest of the card content whenever the line falls inside or near a card
— i.e. mid-chunk `Active`, the normal state during a working day.

## Root cause (from 26-UAT.md — do not re-derive)

`26-UI-SPEC.md` specified the chip **"sized to the hour-axis column width (`52dp`, reusing
`kGutterWidth`)"** *and* gave its content as `"Now · 2:47 PM"`. Those are incompatible: that string
at `labelSmall` 12px/w600 is ~85px of glyphs plus 16dp padding ≈ 101px, which cannot fit 52dp.

`now_line.dart` resolved the contradiction by honouring the string and dropping the width
constraint — `Align(centerLeft)` + `Padding(left: 16)`, no width bound — so the chip spans
x≈16→117. Cards begin at `16dp` inset + `52dp` gutter = **68dp**, so the chip overlaps the card's
leading ~49px, exactly where the title renders. Topmost z-order (correct for the rule) then paints
it over the card.

**Dan's decision, 2026-08-10:** honour the spec's *intent* (a chip in the time column) and change
the string, not the column. Chosen over deleting the chip outright and over widening the gutter to
~101dp (which would cost every row ~12% of a 430px phone's width all day for a marker that matters
at one y).
