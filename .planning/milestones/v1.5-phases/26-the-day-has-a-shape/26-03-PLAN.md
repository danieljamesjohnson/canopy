## Locked planner decisions this plan implements

**PD-7 — `NowMarkerRow` is deleted outright, not kept-and-ignored.** This is **D-01**'s stated
consequence ("`NowMarkerRow`'s between-rows insertion contract is reworked, not extended") taken
literally. Verified independently by `26-RESEARCH.md` (Pattern 1) and `26-PATTERNS.md`: exactly
one `switch` site (`today_screen.dart` `_buildTimelineRow`) and exactly one bare `is` check
(`today_screen.dart`'s `hasMarkerRow`). A sealed variant cannot be retained-but-unused —
exhaustiveness would compel a live, reachable, dead-looking branch in every present and future
switch, which is precisely the parallel-dead-path debt this codebase has spent three phases
removing. Note the direction of the compile risk here is the *opposite* of Phase 24-01's:
*adding* a subtype breaks every switch; *removing* one only requires deleting the dead arm. The
silent risk is the `is` check, which the compiler will not flag — hence the grep gate below.

**PD-8 — `buildTimeline`'s `nowMinutes` parameter survives.** It is load-bearing for NOW-02
(`timeline.dart` line ~103, the `LeadingFreeRow` suppression once its window has closed), a
requirement this phase never revisits. Deleting it alongside the marker would silently regress
NOW-02.

**PD-9 — each row is positioned by its own clock start, not by a cumulative flow.** A `Column`
of duration-sized boxes cannot reproduce clock positions, because `buildTimeline` deliberately
emits no row for gaps under `kMinGapMinutes`. Only absolute positioning against `rangeStart` is
faithful to CAL-01 and to **D-02**'s "an empty 4-hour stretch renders as a real 4-hour-tall
stretch". Consequence: the live row's swell is absorbed by `TimelineGeometry`'s `liveExtraPx`
term (plan 01, PD-2), applied uniformly to every consumer, so rows below the live row and the
hour hairlines below it stay in register.

**PD-10 — every non-live Layer-1 row gets a hard slot height plus an overflow safety net.**
`Positioned(top:, left:, right:, height:)` with a `ClipRect` + `OverflowBox` inside. `OverflowBox`
lets the card lay out at its natural height (so no `RenderFlex` overflow error is ever emitted,
even for pathological data such as a 3-minute work chunk), and `ClipRect` guarantees nothing
paints outside its slot. This is not a min/max clamp and does not violate **D-02**: the slot is
always exactly `durationMinutes * kPixelsPerMinute`, never floored or ceilinged — the safety net
governs only what happens to content that overruns it, and when the plan-02 density tiers are
right, nothing is ever actually clipped. The live row alone is positioned WITHOUT a height, at
its natural size, with `kLiveRowReservedHeight` of space reserved for it; it is appended last
within Layer 1 so that if a future content addition does overrun the reservation it paints over
its neighbour rather than being clipped.

**PD-11 — untimed chunks (`displayStartMinutes == null`) render in a trailing block below the
`Stack`, not inside it.** A chunk with no clock position has no truthful y. The schedule
generator does not currently produce one (`timeline.dart`'s null guard is defensive), so this is
a preservation branch, not a feature.
