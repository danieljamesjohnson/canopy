<objective>
Fix the four allocation-correctness bugs in the scheduling engine so the generated schedule reflects reality rather than processing order. All four fixes live in `lib/services/schedule_generator.dart` Steps 2–4 and are proven by new deterministic unit tests.

- CAP-01: habits cannot consume the entire discretionary cap before outcomes/time-targets are considered (per success criterion SC1).
- PRIORITY-02: priority changes chunk *count* for habits and outcomes, not just sort order (per SC3).
- FILL-01: regular-time goals fill genuinely open capacity on low-mood days instead of leaving the day blank (per SC4).
- FILL-02: open capacity is distributed across regular-time goals by priority via round-robin so no single goal claims the whole open day (per SC5).

Purpose: The engine currently lets habits monopolize a scarce cap, gives every habit/outcome exactly one chunk regardless of priority, hard-gates time-targets off on low-mood days, and greedily fills one time-target fully before the next. This plan makes capacity sharing, priority effect, and day-filling honest.

Output: Modified engine with `habitCeiling`, `habitDemand`, outcome multi-chunk demand, and an always-running round-robin Step 4; four new requirement-tagged engine tests.
</objective>
