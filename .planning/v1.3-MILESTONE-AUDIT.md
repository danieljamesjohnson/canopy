## Cross-Phase Integration (8/8 wired, 3/3 flows)

- **Shared `priorityWeight` model:** written by drag-reorder (0.25–0.75 linear spread) AND the Low/Normal/High selector (exact 0.25/0.5/0.75); read by the engine (habitDemand/outcomeDemand/time-target surplus) and both priority chips at consistent band thresholds (`>= 0.75` High / `<= 0.25` Low). Zero divergent magic numbers.
- **`displayStartMinutes`:** the generator populates `syntheticStartMinutes` for every returned chunk (overflow filtered out), so Phase 17's `resolveNowState` always has real clock windows; the degenerate day-complete fallback is unreachable in production.
- **E2E:** goal `priorityWeight` → engine allocation → `ScheduledChunk` with `syntheticStartMinutes` → `generateToday()` STREAK-01 write-back → persisted schedule → `HomeScreen` `resolveNowState` → time-correct Now/Next. No broken seam.
