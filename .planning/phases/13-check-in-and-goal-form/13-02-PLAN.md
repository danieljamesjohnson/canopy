<objective>
Make the add/edit goal sheet fit the viewport so the Priority selector and Save/Add button are reachable without awkward in-sheet scrolling on a standard phone (GOALFORM-01).

The dogfood frame `0027_02m17s.jpg` showed the `GoalTypePicker` cards (each ~88dp due to `ListTile(contentPadding: EdgeInsets.all(16))` + the default 8dp `minVerticalPadding`) pushing Priority and Save off-screen below the keyboard. The fix is a pure vertical-space-budget reduction:
1. **Compact the type cards (~88dp → ~64dp)** by changing `contentPadding` to `symmetric(horizontal:12, vertical:6)` and adding `minVerticalPadding: 0` (without the latter the change under-delivers — Pitfall 4), plus tightening icon size, border radius, and adding a bold selected-state title.
2. **Reduce three inter-section spacers** in the sheet from 16dp to 12dp.

These two files are disjoint from Plan 01's `checkin_screen.dart`, so this plan runs in parallel (same wave, no shared files).

Purpose: Close GOALFORM-01 — every field including Priority and the Save action must be reachable without clipping.
Output: A compact GoalTypePicker, a tightened goal form sheet, and a layout test guarding the ~64dp card height.
</objective>
