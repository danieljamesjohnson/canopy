## Legacy-Null Behavior Note

Schedules generated before this schema change (schema version 6 and earlier) will read `syntheticStartMinutes` back as `null` from Hive — the Hive CE binary reader returns null for missing fields in pre-existing records. This is the correct and intended behavior per RESEARCH Pitfall 4. Discretionary chunks in legacy schedules will show the `displayStartMinutes` null fallback (duration-only "25 min" text, implemented in plan 12-02) until the next re-check-in regenerates the schedule with the new adapter that now persists field 10.
