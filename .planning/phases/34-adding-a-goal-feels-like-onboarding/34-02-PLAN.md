<objective>
Retire both private copies of the preset-chip idea. Onboarding and the restoratives screen both render
the shared `PresetChipGrid` built in 34-01, so the three surfaces are provably the same widget rather
than three implementations that ought to agree.

Purpose: CONTEXT.md's charter — "a third private copy is the wrong answer," the same delete-the-
duplicate move as D-30-03. If this plan is skipped, the phase ships a THIRD copy and makes the problem
worse.

Output: `_ChipCloud` and `_QuickPickSection`'s private chip construction deleted; onboarding gains the
D-34-04 emoji; the restoratives screen's geometry pinned; the shared widget directly tested in both
modes.

## Two planner decisions this plan takes, with their reasoning

**D-34-07 — the shared widget owns the PRESET GRID only.** `_ChipCloud` does two jobs in one `Wrap`:
it renders removable chips for what you already added, AND suggestion chips for what you have not. The
restoratives screen and the Goals screen each show "what you already have" in their own list
(`_RestorativeRow`s, `GoalCard`s); onboarding has no such list, so `_ChipCloud`'s `InputChip`s are the
only place an onboarding user sees or removes what they just added. Moving onboarding wholesale to
`createOnly` — which HIDES a claimed preset — would delete that display and that removal affordance
outright, leaving beat 1 showing nothing after you add a goal while `Continue` silently enables. So the
extraction line is drawn at the grid: `PresetChipGrid` is the presets, and each surface keeps its own
display of what it has. Onboarding keeps a small private `_AddedChipRow`.

**Resolving a contradiction inside the UI-SPEC, rather than picking silently.** Decision 3 Axis B says
"Goals & onboarding (`createOnly`)"; Decision 5's parenthetical says onboarding's restoratives beat
"also keeps the `toggle` behaviour ... so it doesn't hide claimed items." Both cannot hold. Under
D-34-07 the answer is forced: BOTH onboarding beats use `createOnly`, because the added-chip row
already shows and removes claimed items, and `toggle` would render every claimed item twice — once as
an added `InputChip` and once as a selected `FilterChip`. Decision 5's parenthetical assumed the grid
still carried the added items, which is exactly what D-34-07 changes. The restoratives SCREEN keeps
`toggle` unchanged, which is what Decision 3 actually protects.

**D-34-08 — onboarding's restorative presets become `kCommonRestoratives`.** `_restorativePresets`
(Walk, Music, Nap, Nature, Call a friend, Bath, Stretch, Games) and `kCommonRestoratives` (Walk
outside, Music, Nap, Stretch, Shower, Read, Tea or coffee, Call someone, Sit in the sun) are two
hard-coded lists of the same thing that disagree, and the shared widget requires `(name, emoji)` pairs
either way. Adding emoji to the onboarding list would preserve the disagreement and create a third
list to maintain; it also leaves a live defect, because a user who taps "Walk" in onboarding is later
offered "Walk outside" as an unclaimed chip on the restoratives screen — a near-duplicate the
case-insensitive matcher cannot catch. Onboarding adopts `kCommonRestoratives`. **This is a visible
copy change to a screen the owner did not ask about, and it is called out by name in 34-03's UAT
rather than slipped in.**
</objective>
