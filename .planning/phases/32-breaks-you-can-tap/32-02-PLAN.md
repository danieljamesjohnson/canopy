<objective>
Expand outward from the proven slice: give the long break and the currently-running break the same
card and the same button, then retire every mechanism the button made dead — deliberately, not by
leaving it uncalled.

Purpose: the tracer proved one path. Two more break surfaces exist, and one of them (a live 5-minute
break) would ship with **no way to skip it at all** if the retirement ran without its replacement —
a regression the ROADMAP does not name because it was written before anyone worked through which
live tier a 30dp row reaches. That pairing is why the live rail and the live de-swipe are one task,
not two.

Output: both break tiers on one shape, a live break that can be skipped by a button, and a tree with
no retired mechanism left in it.
</objective>
