The detector fired (`pluralization`, `chosen`). Most signals are prose artifacts, but one real
transition exists and is worth naming: **the way a row is resolved has lost its monopoly.** Before
this phase every chunk row shared one gesture vocabulary; after it, work chunks swipe and breaks tap.

- **Primary noun:** "how a timeline row is resolved" — now plural (gesture for work, button for
  breaks), where it was singular.
- **Decision: `add-alongside`.** `SwipeableChunkCard` stays the single entry point the screen calls,
  with breaks short-circuiting out through the restored early return, rather than promoting the
  general case by making the screen choose `ChunkCard` vs `SwipeableChunkCard` at the call site.
- **Rationale:** this is a revert to the pre-Phase-31 shape that the UI-SPEC specifies verbatim and
  RESEARCH re-verified against the live source. A call-site promotion is a larger refactor than the
  charter, and the early return keeps one entry point for the screen.
- **Accepted debt:** `SwipeableChunkCard`'s name will describe only work chunks while its parameter
  list still says "chunk". What would force a later promote: a third row type needing a third
  resolution vocabulary, or the class acquiring work-chunk-only parameters that make its name
  actively wrong.
- **Companion invariant (accepted, implemented as an acceptance criterion in Task 1):** a break
  never reaches `SwipeableRowShell`. That assertion goes red the instant a future phase reintroduces
  the singular assumption.
