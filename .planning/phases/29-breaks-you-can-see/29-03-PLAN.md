<objective>
Turn `kSubCompactBreakMinHeight` from an honest estimate into a real-browser measurement, and leave
behind a script that can take the measurement again.

Purpose: this is the part of the phase `flutter test` structurally cannot do, and the part this
codebase has got wrong three times — `kGutterWidth` 46→75→52, `kPixelsPerMinute` 4.0→5.5→4.0,
`kCompactLiveMinHeight` 60→84→88. Every one of those was a text-driven number taken from a harness
whose placeholder font has no real Roboto metrics. D-04 exists because of that history.

The subtlety worth stating up front: this constant does **not** measure the new sub-compact tier. It
measures where the *existing, unchanged* `compact` break tier stops fitting — the cutover point away
from it. And the `compact` break tier is unreachable under Phase 28's lattice (a 5-minute break is
20dp, a 30-minute break is 120dp; nothing lands in between), so measuring it needs a deliberate,
temporary mechanism that is then removed.

Output: a measured constant with a house-style doc comment, a committed pixel-count script,
evidence screenshots, and port 8143 claimed as this phase's debug-only port.
</objective>
