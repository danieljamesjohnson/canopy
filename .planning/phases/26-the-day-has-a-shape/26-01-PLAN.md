## Locked planner decisions this plan implements

These were open in `26-RESEARCH.md` / unmeasured in `26-UI-SPEC.md`. They are decided here and
are not to be relitigated downstream.

**PD-1 — `kPixelsPerMinute = 5.5`, superseding the UI-SPEC's `4.0`.** The UI-SPEC picked `4.0`
on the stated ground that "a 25-minute work chunk renders `100px` — enough for a trimmed
**Full**-tier card (title + time range + action row)". That is measurably false. A `ChunkCard`
work card carrying exactly title + time range + the Complete/Skip row, rendered at a width wide
enough that no text wraps, measures **126.0 px** (measured during planning, 2026-08-10, via
`tester.getSize(find.byType(ChunkCard))`). At `4.0` every standard 25-minute chunk would
overflow its slot by 26px. `5.5` gives `137.5px` for 25 minutes — the card fits with ~11px of
slack for real-Roboto line metrics, and a 12-hour day renders `3960px` (≈5 mobile screens),
which is still squarely inside D-02's "the user scrolls through it". Rejected: keeping `4.0` and
dropping the inline Complete/Skip row (an unrequested product regression to the core loop);
keeping `4.0` and clipping (hides an action). This mirrors the `kGutterWidth` 46→75→52
correction precedent — a constant justified by an arithmetic claim gets corrected when the claim
is measured.

**PD-2 — the live row uses a fixed reserved-height constant, not a two-pass measurement**
(`26-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall 3, option (a)). `kLiveRowReservedHeight = 240.0`. `LiveRowCard` with
kicker + title + remaining + progress + actions + next-line measures **230.0 px** (measured
2026-08-10 — the UI-SPEC's "200–220px" estimate was low). The reservation is expressed as a
single `liveExtraPx` term inside `TimelineGeometry`, applied to **every** consumer (rows, hour
axis, now-line, scroll target) at and after the live chunk's end minute — so the one deliberate
CAL-01 exception stretches the whole surface consistently instead of putting rows and hairlines
out of register. Rejected: `GlobalKey`/`RenderBox` measure-then-`setState`, which buys ~10px of
precision at the cost of a correction frame and a whole class of one-frame-snap bugs.

**PD-3 — density tiers are keyed on the row's *pixel slot height*, not on `durationMinutes`.**
The UI-SPEC's "≥ 20 min" threshold was derived from `4.0`; a minute threshold silently rots if
the scale ever changes again. `kFullTierMinHeight = 132.0` (work: 126px content + slack) and
`kFullBreakMinHeight = 88.0` (long break: 80px content measured + slack) express the same intent
in the unit that actually decides fit.
