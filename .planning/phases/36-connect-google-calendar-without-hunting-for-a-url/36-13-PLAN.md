<objective>
**WINDOWS entry 10 was reopened on 2026-11-02**: the owner's screenshot of Settings → Calendars shows no
skipped-events banner at all on re-open. The banner reads `_lastSyncResult`, a field of the screen's
`State`, which is born empty every time the screen is pushed. Plan 36-09 proved the banner's *position*;
nothing ever proved it *survives*. Under D-36-05 this disclosure is the only channel through which the owner
learns his Vacation was not imported, so a disclosure that exists only until he navigates away is not a
disclosure.

This plan is the data half: make the last successful sync's skipped list durable. Plan 36-15 wires both sync
callers (check-in and the Calendars screen) to record it and makes the banner read it.

**Decided here — persistence shape.** Two additive `List<String>` HiveFields on `AppSettings`, written
together by one method: field 16 holds the skipped titles and field 17 holds the matching `SkipReason.name`
values. Chosen over (a) a new Hive type adapter for `SkippedEvent`, which needs a new typeId, adapter
registration in `HiveDatabase.init` and a second generated file for two strings per row; and (b) the
notifier's memory alone, which survives a screen re-open but not iOS killing the app — and "open
Settings → Calendars cold" is exactly how R3 is asked. Two plain lists also stay readable in a raw device
Hive dump, which is how this phase has settled every device finding so far.

**Schema rules, and why they are written down here.** `currentSchemaVersion` is 13. These fields are
additive and are bumped to 14 with a no-op migration, following the per-change convention in
`migrations.dart`. **QUESTIONS.md Q-02 is load-bearing:** the migration comments in that file claim the
Hive reader "returns null/false/0 for missing fields", which is false for a non-nullable field without
`defaultValue:` — `hive_ce_generator` emits an unguarded cast that throws. Both new fields are non-nullable
`List<String>` and therefore MUST carry `defaultValue: <String>[]` (the generator then emits
`fields[N] == null ? [] : ...`, exactly as it does for fields 9 and 10). The new migration comment must name
`defaultValue:` as the reason it is safe and must not repeat the false premise. Do not rewrite the older
migration comments — Q-02 is the owner's open question and proposes its own dedicated plan.

Purpose: a skipped-events disclosure with a memory.
Output: two HiveFields, a schema 13->14 bump, and a `SettingsNotifier` that records and restores the
skipped list.
</objective>
