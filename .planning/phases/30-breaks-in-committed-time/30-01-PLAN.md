<phase_decisions>
Four decisions this phase owns. They are recorded here in full because the tests below encode
them; plans `30-03`/`30-04` implement them and the SUMMARY's `key-decisions` must carry all four.

**D-30-01 — a commitment block runs its OWN cadence counter, fresh per block instance.**
It does not share `breakCount` with `_assignSyntheticStartTimes`'s discretionary loop. Sharing is
not merely more complex, it is structurally unsound: Step 1 executes before Steps 2-4 collect
discretionary demand, but a discretionary chunk can land *chronologically before* a commitment block
(the ROADMAP's own repro puts one at 08:00 ahead of a 09:00 block). A shared counter would therefore
count in code-execution order, not wall-clock order, breaking the "the Nth chunk of your day" model
the cadence exists to serve — and breaking it unpredictably, which contradicts CLAUDE.md's product
position directly ("a schedule the user can't predict is one they won't trust"). Genuine
wall-clock-ordered sharing needs `generate()` restructured into a single interleaved pass, which is
materially larger than the ROADMAP's four deliverables. **Settled on a simulation of the real
packing loop, not an estimate** (the way Phase 28 settled D-04): a 6-hour block at mood 3 (N=4)
yields 10 work + 10 short + 2 long = 360 minutes exactly. Two long breaks for a six-hour meeting is
neither the over-accrual the ROADMAP warns about (four) nor the shipped bug (zero) — it is the same
density six hours of discretionary work already gets at that mood.

**D-30-02 — STEP E's trailing trim narrows to `commitmentId == null`.**
A defect *beyond* the ROADMAP's write-up, found by the research prototype. STEP E trims any trailing
`shortBreak` from the whole day's flat list. Once Step 1 emits breaks, a tail-stretched commitment
break can be the day's last chunk — and trimming it does not remove "5 idle minutes", it removes
however many minutes the stretch absorbed, silently shrinking the covered portion of the user's own
committed window. Observed concretely on the existing `LATTICE-02/D-05` narrow fixture:
`Expected: <45> Actual: <25>` — 20 real committed minutes with no chunk at all. This is a planned
task in `30-03`, not an incidental edit, and `COMMITBREAK-01/STEP-E` below is its regression test.

**D-30-03 — `lib/providers/schedule_notifier.dart:addEventToday` is IN scope: a deliberate scope
extension beyond the ROADMAP.** It carries its own copy of the identical bare `cursor += 25` walk
(lines 282-303) under a doc comment that says it "mirrors the commitment-anchoring step in
ScheduleGeneratorService.generate()". Fixing only the generator would mean a day built at check-in
shows commitment breaks while an event added mid-day from the Today screen does not — reproducing
the owner's exact symptom by a second path immediately after this phase closes. This defect has now
been missed twice by scoping the investigation too narrowly and the owner has reported the symptom
twice; leaving a known second source in place invites a third report. Plans `30-02` and `30-04` own
it, and it is implemented by *sharing* the generator's helper rather than duplicating the loop a
third time.

**D-30-04 — commitment break chunks carry `commitmentId: block.id`.**
Two load-bearing reasons: (1) `commitmentId` is the exact discriminator D-30-02's narrowed STEP E
trim (and `_trimTrailingNonWork`'s narrowing in `30-04`) depends on — without it those trims cannot
tell a commitment break from a discretionary one; (2) attribution honesty, those minutes belong to
the user's committed window. **30-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 6's premise is wrong and is corrected here:**
it claims setting `commitmentId` on a break silently picks up `tertiaryContainer` commitment styling
via `chunk_card.dart:449`. It cannot. `ChunkCard.build()` (`chunk_card.dart:117-135`) switches on
`chunk.chunkType` first and routes `shortBreak`/`longBreak` to `_buildBreak`; `_WorkChunkContent`
— the class whose `build()` holds line 449's `isCommitment` — is constructed **only** under
`case ChunkType.work`. A break chunk never reaches that branch, so `commitmentId` has zero rendering
effect. Plan `30-02` adds `COMMITBREAK-01/RENDER` to prove that rather than assert it. Break chunks
otherwise keep the app's standard break field shape: `goalId: null`, `rationale: ''`.
