CADENCE-SOURCE NOTE (not in the plan's original acceptance criteria list, but
directly supports the "same lattice as a generated day" truth claim)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
addEventToday now sources longBreakEvery from `_todaySchedule?.moodIndex ?? 3`
rather than a hardcoded constant, so a mid-day add onto an already-generated
day uses that day's REAL mood cadence rather than always assuming mood 3. All
of this wave's test fixtures use mood 3 (matching both the plan's acceptance
criteria and the minimal-schedule branch's own pre-existing default), so this
does not change any test's expected numbers — it closes a latent drift vector
where a mood-5 day's cadence (N=5) would otherwise silently disagree between
the generated chunks and any mid-day add on top of them.
