## The invariant this plan installs

A **cell** is 30 minutes. `N = _moodBreakCadence[moodIndex]`, unchanged: `{1:2, 2:3, 3:4, 4:4, 5:5}`.

| Chunk k (discretionary, 1-based) | Reserved footprint | Emitted after the work chunk | Cursor advance |
|---|---|---|---|
| `k % N != 0` | 5 | shortBreak@S+25 (5 min) | 30 |
| `k % N == 0` | 35 | shortBreak@S+25 (5 min), longBreak@S+30 (30 min) | 60 |

Both advances are ≡ 0 (mod 30). Therefore, **if a slot's start is lattice-aligned, every chunk
placed in it is lattice-aligned** — provided the cursor is also realigned in the one case where no
footprint fits (the chunk was placed but its break was not reserved, leaving the cursor at S+25).
That realignment is part of this plan, not an optional tidy-up: without it a chunk placed after an
unreserved trailing chunk lands at `% 30 == 25` and LATTICE-01 fails by a fourth path.

Slot starts are lattice-aligned because: `dayStart` is 480 or `_roundUpToLattice(startFloorMinutes)`
clamped to 480/1320 (all ≡ 0 mod 30), and a post-commitment slot start is
`_roundUpToLattice(windowEnd)`.

A boundary cycle is `N × 30 + 30`. At N=3 that is 120 minutes — back on a clean two-hour boundary.
That is the phase goal, stated as arithmetic, and it is what the tests assert.

## What LATTICE-01 does NOT mean

A short break starts 25 minutes into its cell (`% 30 == 25`). It is the tail of the cell, not a new
one. A blanket "every chunk start `% 30 == 0`" assertion — which both `28-RESEARCH.md` and
`28-PATTERNS.md` sketch — is wrong and would fail on a correct engine. Commitment-anchored chunks
are exempt entirely (D-01). Wave 1's tests already encode the correct per-type invariant; do not
"fix" the engine to satisfy a misremembered version of it.
