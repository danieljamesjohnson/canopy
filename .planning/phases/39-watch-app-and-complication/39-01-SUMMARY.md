# 39-01 Summary

## Shipped
- **Dart:** `WristSync` is debounced, deduplicated on content (`generatedAt` ignored) and listens to both
  notifiers. 6 tests; mutation: 5/5 killed (S1 dedupe, S2 `generatedAt` in the key, S3 goals listener, S4 debounce,
  S5 `hasScheduleToday`).
- **Swift:** the bridge; the watch app's WCSession receiver, which handles the `WKWatchConnectivityRefreshBackgroundTask`
  background wake, saves to the App Group store and reloads the complications; the now screen; complications in
  rectangular, circular, inline and corner families, each with a live `Text(timerInterval:)` and relevance scores.
- **Project:** the targets are generated by a script. CI's iOS job moved to a device `--no-codesign` build.

## Found on the Mac (each one fixed and recorded in the script)
1. `Watch.xcconfig` was referenced relative to `ios/`. Fixed by giving it the `Flutter/` prefix.
2. Flutter's project-level `SUPPORTED_PLATFORMS = iphoneos` compiled the widget against iOS. Fixed with a per-target
   override.
3. `DEBUG` was not defined for the watch Debug config, so the gallery was compiled out.
4. The circular countdown overflowed its ring, and then wrapped onto two lines, because `minimumScaleFactor` does not
   shrink a live timer. Fixed with an explicit 11pt, single-line label (found in team-lead review).
