would otherwise regress silently: that a slop-bearing break wins BOTH slop bands, steals nothing
from a neighbour's own painted content, and that nothing painted moved anywhere on the timeline.

Stack pass — and if someone later "simplifies" that pass back into the chronological loop, every
test in this repository stays green except the one this plan writes. That test is the whole point.

Output: the hit-test proof (both bands plus the negative case) and the painted-grid proof (per
resolved state, adjacency, and total extent).
