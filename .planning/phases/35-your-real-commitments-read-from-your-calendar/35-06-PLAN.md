<objective>
Make an imported commitment legible as imported — on the Commitments screen and on the Today
timeline — and then put it in front of the owner in a browser.

Purpose: an imported commitment that looks identical to a hand-entered one invites the user to edit
it, and the next sync silently undoes the edit. The glyph and the read-only sheet are what keep the
read-only guarantee honest at the surface rather than only in the data layer.

Output: the `_CommitmentRow` imported treatment, the timeline glyph, the render-time lookup that
carries `isFromCalendar` to the card without a schema change, and `35-UAT.md`.

## D-35-11 — why a lookup, not a field on ScheduledChunk

The design contract flagged this as a data-flow decision for planning. It is decided here, with the
reasoning recorded, because the wrong answer is invisible until it bites.

Adding `isFromCalendar` to `ScheduledChunk` would mean a third Hive schema bump this phase — and
worse, it would walk straight into CLAUDE.md's trap #4: an already-generated day is never regenerated
on load, so every day already sitting in Hive would deserialize the new field as its default and show
no glyph, forever, until the user happened to re-check-in. The served bytes would be correct, the code
would be correct, and the screen would be wrong — the most convincing failure shape this repo has.

A render-time lookup of `chunk.commitmentId` against `CommitmentsNotifier.blocks`, in
`today_screen._buildChunkCard`, has neither problem. It also lands exactly where the file already does
this: `_lookupGoalColor`, `_lookupGoalName`, `_lookupGoalPriorityWeight`, `_lookupGoalEmojiTag` and
`_lookupGoalValence` are all render-time lookups against a notifier, in that same method.

## Who verifies this

Tasks 1 and 2 are `flutter analyze` plus widget tests, entirely on danserver. Task 3 is the owner, in
a browser over the tailnet — the surface this phase has been building toward, and the one he can
actually reach from his phone.
</objective>
