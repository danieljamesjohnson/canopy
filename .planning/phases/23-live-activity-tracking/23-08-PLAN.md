<objective>
Close two UAT gaps on surfaces outside the Today screen.

**G-01 (polish).** The energy-valence 3-way control reads `Lifts | Neutral | Drains`; Dan wants
`Drains | Neutral | Lifts`. His decision at the sign-off gate was explicit: **flip both surfaces** —
onboarding beat 3 *and* the analogous control in `goal_form_sheet.dart` — so the two never disagree
about which end means "drains". Flipping only onboarding would create a new inconsistency where
today there is at least agreement on order.

**G-07 (design).** Choosing a day type doesn't explain its consequence. Dan: *"If you choose a day
type, it should tell you what that means (high chunk count, low break chunk count) or vice versa on
a hard day."* The copy must be accurate to the engine's real mapping — `_moodCap` and
`_moodBreakCadence` in `lib/services/schedule_generator.dart` — not a vibe. No new screen.

Purpose: the app's pitch is control over your own time. A control whose polarity flips between
screens, and a mood picker that hides what it does, both undercut that.
Output: two reordered controls with positional tests, and one honest sentence per mood shown at the
moment of choosing.
</objective>
