<objective>
Make defer-to-tomorrow actually carry over (CLOSE-02).

Switch `markDeferred` to log `CompletionEvent.deferred` instead of Phase 8's log-as-skipped, make
`computeStreak` treat deferred days as non-breaking (a move, not a miss), give the generator a
`deferredGoalIds` input, and have `generateToday` pull the immediately-preceding day's unresolved
deferred *discretionary* chunks and re-materialize them as fresh demand for the same goal through the
normal generator.

Purpose: A deferred chunk currently does nothing — it is logged as skipped and disappears. CLOSE-02
makes deferral a real "do it tomorrow" action: the goal re-enters the next morning's generation (so it
respects today's ordering, breaks, and capacity rather than copying a stale chunk), and deferring does
not punish a habit streak. Single-hop only — no unbounded backlog.
Output: deferred event logging, non-breaking-streak handling, `deferredGoalIds` generator input, and
single-hop carry-in lookup in `generateToday`, plus regression tests for re-appearance and streak
survival.
</objective>
